V3.6.6 sprite mapping audit

Sprite policy:
- Existing local Cobblemon sprite mappings remain the primary source.
- When no normal local Cobblemon sprite exists, the fallback uses Pokémon Showdown HOME sprites.
- Showdown-specific slug aliases are applied for Paradox Pokémon and other species whose Showdown filenames omit hyphens.
- Form-specific aliases are applied for Deoxys, Dudunsparce and Gimmighoul where the LivingDex identifier differs from the Showdown filename.
- Shiny fallback uses the matching Showdown HOME shiny sprite path.

Verified Showdown HOME fallback aliases include:
Great Tusk, Scream Tail, Brute Bonnet, Flutter Mane, Slither Wing, Sandy Shocks,
Iron Treads, Iron Bundle, Iron Hands, Iron Jugulis, Iron Moth, Iron Thorns,
Roaring Moon, Iron Valiant, Walking Wake, Iron Leaves, Gouging Fire, Raging Bolt,
Iron Boulder, Iron Crown, Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, Ting-Lu, Chi-Yu,
Deoxys Attack/Defense/Speed, Dudunsparce, and Gimmighoul Roaming.
